Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Everything ships in the app and your progress stays on your iPhone.

Why don't I see "Hvorfor var mit svar forkert?" or AI writing feedback? They use Apple's on-device model and need iOS 26 on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. The rest of the app works without them.

How do I switch explanations between English and Danish? Tap EN / DA on any explanation, or change it in Settings.

How do I start over? Settings → Nulstil al fremgang.
